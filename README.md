A 13.9-second vertical Short cut from Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI
Trailer 2. Built for the $500 gta6-clipper-01 bounty on make.fun.

## The video

- **Hook (0–3 s):** Jason on a red motorcycle against a blue sky, seaplane
  behind him — slowed down, with giant on-screen text: **"THIS IS A
  VIDEO GAME"**. The claim is the hook: what looks real is a game.
- **Montage (3–8 s):** jet ski chase, helicopter over the swamp at sunset,
  then the Vice City skyline at golden hour with giant **"VICE CITY"** text.
- **Payoff (8–14 s):** Muscle Beach bench press, then a highway POV drive
  under a flying plane while the make.fun pitch runs as a text overlay:
  "Make clips like this with AI / and earn money on make.fun".

## Why this cut

The worldwide top-viewed GTA 6 clips win with **giant text from the first
frame** and a **claim + visual proof**, not with pretty footage alone.
v1 and v2 had no text on the hook — just beautiful shots — and felt flat.
v3 puts the text first: the viewer reads the impossible claim, then the
footage proves it.

## Files

- `VIDEO.json` — the published YouTube Shorts URL (filled in after upload)
- `main.ts` — copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output` (Node 22, stdlib only)
- `clip-v3.mp4` — the rendered clip (1080×1920, 13.9 s, H.264 + AAC;
  kept out of this repo, uploaded to YouTube separately)
